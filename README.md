# Macau Industry Concentration (HHI), 2015–2024

Reproduce Macau’s sectoral concentration from official DSEC industrial-structure shares: gaming vs non-gaming GDP share, Herfindahl–Hirschman Index (HHI) under 7- and 16-sector classifications, and trend charts.

## Question

How did Macau’s economic concentration in gaming evolve from 2015 to 2024, including the COVID period and the post-pandemic rebound?

## Data

- `data/sector_shares_allyears.json` — producer-price value-added shares (%) by 16 sectors, 2015–2024, transcribed from DSEC *Industrial Structure* reports (`SC_PIBP`).
- `data/HHI_full_decade.csv` — derived gaming share and HHI series (regenerated by the script).

Always cite DSEC as the primary source; this repo only aggregates and computes indices.

## Method

HHI = Σ sᵢ² where sᵢ is each sector’s share in **percent** (same convention as many published Macau structure tables). Higher HHI ⇒ more concentrated. A common reference line is **2,500** (high concentration under this percent-based scale).

16 DSEC sectors are also rolled into 7 groups (Gaming; Finance; Public admin / education / health / other; Real estate; Construction; Wholesale & retail; Others) for a coarser robustness check.

## Run

python -m venv .venv
source .venv/bin/activate   # Windows: .venv\Scripts\activate
pip install -r requirements.txt

Outputs refresh `data/HHI_full_decade.csv` and `figures/gaming_hhi_trend.png`, `figures/structure_area.png`.

## Findings (from the 2015–2024 series)

- Gaming share rose from **48.0% (2015)** to **50.9% (2019)**; 7-sector HHI moved **2803 → 3068**.
- 2020–2022: gaming collapsed with border closures (HHI fell as low as **1577**).
- 2023–2024: gaming rebounded to **43.3%**; HHI rose again to **2527**, back above the 2,500 line.

Interpretation caveat: share metrics move with the **denominator**. A fall in gaming’s *share* can reflect gaming’s collapse as much as non-gaming growth—pair shares with absolute value-added or employment when drawing policy conclusions.

## Limitations

- Shares are transcribed from published tables; always re-check against the latest DSEC PDF if figures are revised.
- Aggregation into 7 categories is an author choice; results are sensitive to grouping.
- HHI on percent shares is not comparable one-for-one to HHI defined on 0–1 fractions without rescaling.
